## C. Independently confirmed (no change needed)

* BUG-M06 fix: the horizon-free predictable schedule and the predeclared fixed horizon are valid. 400,000 random adversarial cases found no counterexample to the per-step inequality E[exp(λ(m − X) − 4ψ_E(λ)(X − μ̂)²)] ≤ 1 (`audit_checks/cs_check.py`); coverage of the running-minimum UCB was ≤ 0.43% against a nominal 2.5% on seven hard streams.
* Spectral support: U_spec ≥ brute-force MaxCut on all 68 graphs rebuilt from seeds; within ~1% of MaxCut on weighted graphs (mean 0.9%, max 3.4%), exact after flooring on unit-weight graphs.
* Resource accounting: search + confirmation ≤ total budget in every Phase 6 and Phase 7 trial.
* Shipped SHA-256 manifest matched the shipped files (the one mismatch seen during the audit was caused by re-running a test that regenerates `phase3_synthetic_calibration.csv`; the original was restored).
